# omni-sql v0.22.0

- Editable environments: open **Environments…** from the connection form or from Manage connections to create your own environments (for example QA or Homologation), rename them, pick a color, reorder them or remove them. Development, Staging and Production remain as defaults and can be renamed or recolored; **Restore defaults** brings them back. The list is saved on this computer and is not included in connection exports.
- Environment badges now use the environment's color, so Development and Staging appear in green and yellow instead of blue.
- If you remove an environment that connections use, those connections show no environment until you choose another one.
- Connections and folders are now listed in alphabetical order (ignoring case and accents, with numbers in natural order, so `db2` comes before `db10`). Recent connections keep their most-recently-used order.

**Full Changelog:** [v0.21.1...v0.22.0](https://github.com/cccadet/omni-sql/compare/v0.21.1...v0.22.0)
