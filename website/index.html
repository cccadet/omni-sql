Import, export, and local storage details

Preview CSV before importing

Choose comma, semicolon, Tab, or a custom delimiter and preview up to 20 rows before a local CSV import. Choose a delimiter for CSV exports, too.

Keep track of what you imported

Choose a full query result or a sample. Full loads are subject to local resource budgets; samples retain only the selected rows. A full export includes the rows available in your imported datasets and cannot restore omitted rows.

Local DuckDB datasets persist after restart. Full analytical exports include a companion provenance file. Import coverage and provenance.

Work with S3 data

Browse and query supported S3 objects, then import them locally when you need a full analytical export. S3 CSV imports use automatic delimiter detection; additional formats depend on DuckDB extensions.
