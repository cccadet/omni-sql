Import, export, and local storage details

Preview before importing

Preview up to 20 CSV rows and choose comma, semicolon, Tab, or a custom delimiter. CSV exports also support a chosen delimiter.

Keep full loads and samples distinct

Choose a full query result or a sample. Full loads are subject to local resource budgets; samples retain only selected rows. A full export includes the rows in your imported datasets and cannot restore omitted rows.

Local DuckDB datasets persist after restart. Full analytical exports include a companion provenance file. Import coverage and provenance.
